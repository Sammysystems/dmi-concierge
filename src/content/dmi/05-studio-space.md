# Studio Space at DMI

Studio space in Uyo can be booked by anyone, with or without a DMI photographer. It is rented in 30 minute slots.

## Spaces
- Backdrop, no air conditioning: 8,000 naira per 30 minutes
- Backdrop with air conditioning: 10,000 naira per 30 minutes
- Content Space: 20,000 naira per 30 minutes

## Studio space extras
- Extra 30 minutes: charged at the same price as the space chosen
- Props: 5,000 naira each
- Flower Pots: 5,000 naira each
- Chairs: 5,000 naira each

## How a studio space is priced
Multiply the space price by the number of 30 minute slots, then add any extras. For example, two slots of the backdrop with air conditioning plus four chairs is 20,000 plus 20,000, which is 40,000 naira.

## What a studio space booking needs
Full name, WhatsApp number, email if they want one, the rental date, the rental time, which space, how many 30 minute slots, any extras, and notes. Default start time offered is 10:00.

## Good to know
The space is charged by the half hour, so tell clients how long they think they need before you add up a total. The content space is the one to suggest for short form video and creator work. The backdrop with air conditioning is the comfortable one for longer portrait sessions.
