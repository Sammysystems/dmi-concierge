# Weddings at DMI

DMI has a separate weddings feed and an Instagram for wedding work. Wedding coverage is sold as a package, with a one event price and a two event price.

## Packages
- Pearl: 600,000 naira for one event, 800,000 naira for two events
- Sapphire: 800,000 naira for one event, 1,000,000 naira for two events
- Emerald: 1,000,000 naira for one event, 1,200,000 naira for two events. Emerald is the featured package.
- Diamond: 1,200,000 naira for one event, 1,400,000 naira for two events
- Court Marriage: 100,000 naira, one price
- Introduction: 100,000 naira, one price

A two event wedding is simply the wedding day plus the traditional or reception day. Ask whether the client wants one event or two, then recommend a single package that fits their day and their budget. Do not list every package unless they ask for the full range.

Court marriage and introduction are small, single day ceremonies with one photographer and cost 100,000 naira.

## Wedding add-ons
- Drone: 50,000 naira
- Extra photographer per event: 100,000 naira
- Extra videographer per event: 100,000 naira
- Backdrops: 70,000 naira
- Content creator: 150,000 naira

## How to price a wedding
Take the package price for the number of events, then add any extras the client chose. The total is the package plus the extras.

## Payment terms
A 70% deposit is required to hold the date and it is non-refundable. The 30% balance is due within 3 days of the wedding. Quote these terms only when the client asks about payment.

## What a wedding booking needs
Name, partner's name, email, WhatsApp number, wedding date, venue, city, whether it is one event or two, extras, and notes. The chat only needs a name, a WhatsApp number, that it is a wedding and the city to record the request.
