# Portrait Sessions at DMI

A portrait session is quoted on three things: the city, whether it is indoors or outdoors, and how many people are in the frame.

## Cities
There are three portrait price tiers: Uyo, Lagos and Abuja. Lagos and Abuja share one tier. Ask the client which city they are shooting in first, then indoor or outdoor, then individual, couple or family.

## Uyo portrait prices
The Uyo session rate is 1 outfit with 3 edited images.

- Individual indoor: 50,000 naira
- Couple indoor: 60,000 naira
- Family indoor: 70,000 naira
- Individual outdoor: 70,000 naira
- Couple outdoor: 80,000 naira
- Family outdoor: 90,000 naira

## Lagos and Abuja portrait prices
These are session rates.

- Individual indoor: 100,000 naira
- Couple indoor: 120,000 naira
- Family indoor: 150,000 naira
- Individual outdoor: 150,000 naira
- Couple outdoor: 170,000 naira
- Family outdoor: 200,000 naira

## Portrait add-ons
- 30-second Reel: 40,000 naira
- Extra Edited Picture: 10,000 naira each, maximum 20
- Extra Outfit: charged at the same price as the package
- All Unedited Photos: 15,000 naira in Uyo, 20,000 naira in Lagos and Abuja

## What a portrait booking needs
The booking form asks for the shoot type (portrait, pre-wedding, birthday, graduation, fashion or editorial, product, event, corporate, family), gender, the city, indoor or outdoor, the date and time, outfits, social handles, inspiration links, and any notes. Default start time offered is 10:00.

## Popular portrait occasions
Pre-wedding, birthday, graduation, fashion and editorial, product, corporate and family sessions all run on the same portrait rate card.
