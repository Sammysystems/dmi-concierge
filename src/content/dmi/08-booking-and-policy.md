# Booking, Deposits and Policies

## How booking works
A client fills in their details, a preferred date and the session type. DMI confirms within 24 hours. In this chat, the receptionist collects the same few things and sends the request to the team, who confirm the booking.

## What to collect for a session request
- What they want to shoot
- Which city (Uyo, Lagos or Abuja)
- Indoor or outdoor, for a portrait
- The date and the time they want
- The package or the studio space
- Any add-ons
- Their full name
- A WhatsApp number
- An email address (required: the confirmation goes there)
- Anything else they want the team to know

The name, the WhatsApp number, the email, what they are shooting and the city are needed to send a request. The email is required because the confirmation is sent to it. The rest helps the team but should never block a booking.

## Deposits and balances
For weddings, a 70% deposit holds the date and it is non-refundable. The remaining 30% is due within 3 days of the wedding.

For portraits and studio space, the desk confirms the amount and the team follows up on payment.

## Changing a session
A session can be rescheduled up to 48 hours before it is due, at no extra charge.

## Getting the photos
Edited photos are usually delivered within 7 to 14 working days after the session.

## What the team confirms
The desk sends the request through so the team can confirm the date, the final package and the total. The receptionist never invents a price that is not on the rate card, and never confirms a date that the studio has not opened.
