# DarlMac Impression — Overview

DarlMac Impression is known as DMI. It is a Nigerian photography studio founded and led by Macauley Nseh, who is the founder, creative director and lead photographer. The studio turns fleeting moments into printed imagination.

The studio has delivered more than 300 projects over seven years, with an average client rating of 5.0. DMI works out of Uyo, Lagos and Abuja, and shoots weddings, maternity stories, birthdays, editorials and fine-art portraits across Lagos, Uyo and beyond.

DMI's work is recognised for cinematic light, deep skin tones and stillness. The studio blends classical portraiture with a modern editorial sensibility. On a shoot, Macauley directs gently, listens carefully and waits for the half-second when a person finally exhales into the frame.

What the studio promises: industry-level expertise with precision, award-winning creative work, more than 95% of clients achieving better brand recognition and results, and flexible, transparent pricing that delivers value without compromise.

The studio also runs the DMI Photography Academy in Uyo, a three-month immersive programme that mentors the next generation of Nigerian photographers in lighting, business, branding and storytelling. Studio space is available to rent by the half hour.
