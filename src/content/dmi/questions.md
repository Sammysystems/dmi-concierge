# Questions Clients Ask

## How does the booking process work?
Fill out the booking details with your name, preferred date and session type. DMI gets back within 24 hours to confirm. In the chat, the receptionist takes the same details and sends them to the team.

## What types of photography do you offer?
Portrait, wedding, event, fashion, product and commercial photography. DMI also shoots maternity, birthdays, graduations, pre-wedding and family sessions, and covers videography.

## How much does a session cost?
The desk quotes real prices. Portraits start at 50,000 naira in Uyo and 100,000 naira in Lagos and Abuja. Weddings run from 600,000 naira for the Pearl package. Studio space starts at 8,000 naira per 30 minutes. Ask the receptionist for the exact figure that fits the session.

## Can I reschedule my session?
Yes. You can reschedule up to 48 hours before the session at no additional charge.

## How long until I get my photos?
Edited photos are usually delivered within 7 to 14 working days after the session.

## Which cities do you shoot in?
Uyo, Lagos and Abuja. The studio space and the academy are in Uyo.

## Can I rent the studio without a photographer?
Yes. Studio space in Uyo is rented by the 30 minute slot, and you can bring your own photographer or shoot yourself.

## Do you cover weddings outside your three cities?
The studio covers weddings in Uyo, Lagos and Abuja. For anywhere else, leave your number and the team will tell you if they can travel.

## Can I learn photography with DMI?
Yes. The DMI Photography Academy in Uyo runs for three months on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, for 200,000 naira in full or 100,000 naira in two installments.

## How do I pay a deposit?
For a wedding, a 70% deposit holds the date and is non-refundable, with the 30% balance due within 3 days of the wedding. The desk passes the request to the team, who send the payment details.
