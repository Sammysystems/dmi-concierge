# Services at DMI

DMI offers photography and film under six headings. Everything can be booked as a session request through this chat.

## Photography
Studio and on-location photography: portraits, weddings, events, maternity, birthdays, graduations, fashion and editorial, product, corporate and family work. Portraits are quoted by location, setting and how many people are in the frame.

## Portraits
Individual, couple and family sessions, shot indoors in the studio or outdoors on location. Portrait prices depend on the city and the setting. Each session comes with edited images, and add-ons like a short reel, extra edited pictures or the full unedited set can be added.

## Videography
Film coverage alongside stills. Weddings can include an extra videographer per event for 100,000 naira, and a drone for 50,000 naira. Portrait sessions can add a 30-second reel for 40,000 naira.

## Events
Event photography for birthdays, launches, corporate days and private parties, shot in Uyo, Lagos or Abuja.

## Studio Space
Self-service studio space in Uyo, rented by the 30 minute slot: a backdrop without air conditioning for 8,000 naira, a backdrop with air conditioning for 10,000 naira, and a content space for 20,000 naira. Props, flower pots and chairs are 5,000 naira each.

## Academy
The DMI Photography Academy in Uyo: a three month programme covering lighting, business, branding and storytelling, taught on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. It costs 200,000 naira in full, or 100,000 naira in two installments.

## Store
Digital and print products ship to buyers: Lightroom presets for 8,500 naira, the Posing Guide print book "Printed Imagination" for 5,500 naira, and the mini-course "Photography as a Business" for 15,000 naira.
