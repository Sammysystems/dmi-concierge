# DMI Photography Academy

The DMI Photography Academy is a three month immersive programme in Uyo for people who want to shoot professionally. It teaches lighting, business, branding and storytelling.

## Batches
- Batch A: January to March
- Batch B: May to July
- Batch C: September to November

Classes run on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays.

## Fees and payment
- Full payment: 200,000 naira
- Two installments: 100,000 naira each

Payment is made to FCMB, account number 2008197378.

## What the academy asks for
The application form has five steps: personal details (full name, email, phone, guardian phone, state of origin, date of birth, social handle, and the batch they want), experience level (background, whether they have trained before, and whether they own gear), learning goal, session and payment choice, and terms. There is a guardian phone field, so the academy takes students of school age too.

## Good to know
The academy is only in Uyo. If someone is outside Uyo, tell them that honestly and point them to the studio space or to a session instead. Batches fill up, so if someone is sure, the thing to do is get their name and WhatsApp so the team can follow up on the next intake.

## Recording an academy request
The chat records a session request the same way for the academy: the person's name, a WhatsApp number, that the enquiry is about the academy, and their city. The team follows up with the application form and the next batch dates.
